# SteelThumb Almanac

The Almanac describes reusable growing knowledge. Shared guidance retains the evidence and conditions that make it applicable; a particular garden's operational vocabulary belongs to SteelThumb.

## Language

**Crop**: A practical category of plants grown for a purpose. Its scope may cover one species or several related plants; it is distinct from a cultivar and a particular planting.

**Crop profile**: Reusable knowledge about an explicitly scoped crop, including identity, growing guidance, uses, and links to procedures. It does not represent a particular planting.

**Cultivar**: A named cultivated selection whose identity and guidance may be more specific than the broader crop profile. An informal name or supplier product name does not by itself establish cultivar identity.

**Procedure**: Reusable instructions for performing a task. Following a procedure is a separate event from documenting it.

**Symptom**: An observable sign used to describe a concern. A symptom alone does not identify its cause.

**Claim**: An assertion about a subject whose evidence and applicability can be examined independently of surrounding prose.

**Source**: An identifiable material used as evidence for a claim, such as a study, supplier instruction, or observation record.

**Applicability**: The conditions under which a claim or procedure is intended to hold, such as cultivar, growth stage, growing system, and climate.

**Field report**: A contributor's published account of an actual gardening attempt, preserving what they did, observed, and concluded, with the conditions and limitations needed to interpret it. It is a selected contribution rather than the contributor's entire garden journal.

**Attempt**: A particular occasion on which someone carried out gardening work, including any deviations from the procedure they intended to follow. Several plants in one attempt do not necessarily constitute independent replications.

**Outcome**: An observed result at a stated follow-up time, assessed against a stated objective when available. Success, failure, partial results, and an unknown or pending result remain distinguishable.

**Evidence**: Source material bearing on a specific claim, with an explicit supporting, contradicting, or qualifying relationship. A report that a procedure was used is distinct from evidence that it achieved an outcome or caused it.

**Provenance**: The recorded origins and history of knowledge, including the sources, attempts, contributors, and revisions involved. Provenance supports inspection without guaranteeing truth.

**Review**: A recorded check of an identified claim, report, or procedure revision, with a reviewer, scope, basis, and result. Reviewing a report does not mean independently repeating the reported attempt.

**Tip**: A concise practical recommendation with explicit applicability and supporting evidence or a stated evidence gap. A tip can begin as a hypothesis derived from one field report.

**Food profile**: The composition of an identified food part in a specified preparation state and mass basis. It may represent a generic food rather than a named cultivar.

**Edible yield**: The harvested mass retained as food after stated exclusions and losses. It is distinct from total plant biomass and gross harvest mass.

**Caloric yield**: Estimated food energy represented by an edible yield, optionally expressed per growing area and stated time period. It is not energy density or net energy after cultivation costs.

**Nutrient yield**: The amount of a specified nutrient represented by an edible yield, calculated from matching food composition or measured directly.
