# Information coverage and research strategies

Use this as a coverage map, not a demand to fill every cell. Prefer a useful supported answer to the gardener's question over a long list of uncertain numbers. Preserve missing knowledge explicitly.

## What to gather

| Topic | Information worth gathering | Context that changes its meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity and purpose | Common/scientific names, accepted names and synonyms, edible parts, lifecycle, crop scope | Species versus cultivar, hybrids, informal market groups, rootstock/scion, plant part |
| Cultivars and planting material | Supported flavor/habit/maturity/resistance differences; seed, tuber, cutting, transplant, graft, or division | Named selection versus supplier product/lot; resistance to a particular pathogen or strain; sexual versus vegetative propagation |
| Establishment | Starting method, seed treatment if supported, sowing depth, germination conditions, emergence, transplant readiness, acclimation, spacing | Temperature of air versus medium, starting event, in-row versus between-row distance, plants per container, cultivar and seed lot |
| Growing conditions | Light, temperature, water, drainage, medium, fertility, pH, salinity where relevant | Measured light versus lamp distance; frost exposure; indoor/outdoor; medium and pH method; actual fertilizer formulation and concentration |
| Lifecycle and seasonal care | Growth stages, pollination, flowering/fruiting, support, pruning, thinning, dormancy, overwintering, succession | Growing objective, region/season, rootstock, chilling/daylength requirements where relevant; not every crop follows an annual seed-to-leaf cycle |
| Harvest | Readiness, edible part, technique, recovery, repeated harvest, quality criteria | Days from sowing versus transplanting, maturity versus first pick, plant condition, fresh/dry and edible/gross mass |
| Troubleshooting | Symptoms, candidate causes, distinguishing checks, supported interventions, follow-up | Similar symptoms with different causes, disease identity, growth stage, cultivar susceptibility, local conditions |
| Propagation and seed saving | Cuttings/division/grafting as applicable; seed maturation, pollination, isolation, storage, viability | Sterile selections, hybrid offspring, cultivar fidelity, actual germination tests, species-specific methods |
| Food use and preservation | Appropriate edible parts, preparation state, supported storage/preservation methods | Freshness versus safety; raw/cooked/dried; recipe and process conditions; different parts of the same plant |
| Nutrition | Source food name/ID, energy and nutrients with units, preparation and mass basis | Generic composition versus cultivar assay; missing values versus zero; composition is not a health effect |
| Yield and resource use | Measured or explicitly modeled harvest, area, density, duration, losses, input basis | Single harvest versus season; edible mass versus biomass; trial setting; a scenario is not an observed garden outcome |
| Tips and experience | Actual steps, before/after observations, outcome, limitations, failures | Procedure revision, deviations, contributor attribution, attempt identity, repeated observations versus independent attempts |

Prioritize the parts necessary for the requested growing task. Perennial fruit, leafy annuals, roots/tubers, and seed crops may need very different procedures. If numerical guidance is absent, describe the missing evidence rather than filling the field with a related crop's value.

## Choose evidence for the question

| Question | Start with | Check before using it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What plant does this name identify? | Botanical authorities and crop-specific institutional references | Accepted name, synonyms, rank, record identifier, and whether cultivar identity is actually covered |
| How is this crop grown in this setting? | Regional extension or public agricultural guidance | Region, intended audience/scale, date, system, and actual source of the recommendation |
| Does a cultivar differ or an intervention work? | Original trials, breeding/release records, and research papers | Methods, comparator, sample/replication, cultivar, environment, measured endpoint, uncertainty, and limitations |
| What does this seed product instruct? | The exact supplier packet or product document | Product identity, date/edition, measurement basis; it establishes that product's instructions, not universal superiority |
| What might explain this symptom? | Diagnostic references, extension/IPM resources, and relevant research | Distinguishing evidence, alternative causes, host scope; a photograph alone is not laboratory confirmation |
| What nutrients are in this food? | A primary food-composition database or assay | Food ID, data type/release, edible part, preparation, mass basis, values and missingness |
| How can this food be preserved? | An applicable primary food-preservation authority and tested method | The exact preparation, process, conditions, and current instructions; do not combine fragments into a newly implied tested recipe |
| What happened for this gardener? | Their original account and supplied records | Firsthand versus relay, conditions, dates, deviations, counts, outcomes, publication agreement, and shared attempt identity |
| How is a physical technique performed? | An attributable demonstration plus relevant technical guidance | Actual video segment/timestamp; a demonstration shows an action, not its comparative effectiveness |

Peer review, institutional publication, and firsthand experience answer different questions. A single well-matched source can support a narrow assertion; two unrelated URLs do not automatically corroborate it. Seek independent evidence for consequential or contested generalizations rather than impose a universal source quota.

## Search in passes

1. **Orient:** search the common name and scientific name; inspect an identity reference and a relevant growing guide. Extract the questions and citations worth pursuing.
2. **Target gaps:** search a particular property, stage, cultivar, and system. Query patterns include `"<crop>" "<region>" extension planting`, `"<scientific name>" "<cultivar>" trial`, and `"<crop>" "<system>" <property> study`. Domain filters help narrow to an identified institution but `.edu` alone does not establish evidence quality.
3. **Trace the claim:** follow a guide's references to the original study, table, supplier instruction, or observation when the claimed precision or inference requires it. Record inaccessible originals as such.
4. **Challenge the conclusion:** look for different conditions, negative findings, conflicting ranges, and method limitations. Check whether apparent disagreement disappears when starting event, cultivar, system, or units are made explicit.
5. **Stop deliberately:** when the requested questions have adequate scoped support, draft. If focused searches produce only repeated unsourced claims, retain a stated gap or hypothesis and identify the observation/research that would resolve it. More retellings do not improve the evidence.

Open actual pages/PDFs and inspect relevant figures/tables when needed. Preserve the table's headings and footnotes while extracting values. For videos, inspect the relevant segment or a reliable transcript and cite the timestamp; do not infer the content from the title. For datasets, preserve record identity and a permitted snapshot when the calculation depends on exact values.

Use blogs, forums, vendor marketing, and aggregators as leads or explicitly scoped original accounts. Promote their claims only on evidence appropriate to the assertion. A supplier is a primary source for its own instructions, not necessarily independent evidence of a performance claim.

## Evidence notes during research

For each candidate claim, capture:

```text
Proposed statement / gardener question:
Source ID, title, author or institution, URL/DOI/record ID:
Source version/publication date if supplied; actual access date:
Inspected passage, page/table/section/timestamp:
Original value, unit, basis, and any conversion:
Cultivar, stage, system, climate/season, and other conditions:
Study/guide/product instruction/firsthand account/demonstration:
Supports, contradicts, or qualifies which assertion:
Limitations, linked original study/attempt, and reuse terms:
```

These are research notes, not an additional mandatory frontmatter schema. Use the repository's established fields in final documents; put information without an agreed field in readable source notes. Keep access date distinct from publication date, source modification date, and claim review date. Never invent them from a web crawler's timestamp.

For disagreements, keep separate claims and their applicability. Convert units transparently, but do not average conflicting recommendations or present their endpoints as one measured range. If selecting one for a particular procedure, state why it fits that scope and link the alternatives.

For an unsourced tip, identify a falsifiable outcome: what would the gardener observe, when, and compared with what if a comparison is feasible? A suggested experiment remains a proposal, and designing operational experiment contracts belongs in SteelThumb rather than this reference bundle.

## Starting points

The following official entry points were consulted on 2026-09-27. Use them to find specific records; cite the record supporting a claim rather than the directory homepage. Prefer appropriate institutions in the user's region; these examples do not make United States conditions the default.

- [Kew Plants of the World Online](https://powo.science.kew.org/): botanical names, synonyms, and plant records.
- [USDA GRIN Taxonomy](https://npgsweb.ars-grin.gov/gringlobal/taxon/taxonomysearch): taxonomic search, including common/scientific names and hybrid information.
- [UC IPM crop guidelines](https://ipm.ucanr.edu/agriculture/): crop-specific pest-management references; preserve their geographic and production scope.
- [USDA FoodData Central](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/): food-composition records; inspect the specific food and data documentation.
- [National Center for Home Food Preservation](https://nchfp.uga.edu/): food-preservation guidance; use the actual applicable method rather than generalizing from another recipe.

Refresh changeable instructions when researching. A current retrieval does not make an old trial contemporary, and an old primary study may still be relevant when its scope and limitations are retained.
