## Changes in this edition

Reorganized the cultivation compilation into crop profiles, growing/variety guides, task-oriented procedures, and a system description. Consolidated task fragments, exposed competing parameter assumptions, and rewrote the learning guide to link to maintained procedures. Replaced the video essay with a source catalog. Normalized attribution into source IDs and footnotes on the new documents. No local copy of the old basil import is retained.

Unsupported diagnostic shortcuts, preservation recipes, guaranteed yields, and overgeneralized taxonomy/flowering assertions are not promoted to established advice. Review topics remain in the relevant documents and original wording is accessible through the pinned sources. Robot models and execution policies remain in SteelThumb.

The old image is not redistributed in this edition because its separate creator and reuse terms were unresolved. Its source path and historical hash are retained for attribution research. This is not a finding about ownership of the image.

## Evidence limits

Neither the old compilation nor the task catalog supplies actual gardening attempts, field outcomes, or a completed source review. No `verified` event is claimed. Original citations are leads, and the old phrase “expert validation” is not an Almanac review record. Source availability and horticultural accuracy remain to be checked independently.
