The card's numerical summaries were compared with their linked claims. Cultivar-dependent maturity and compact-plant spacing remain decisions for the actual plant label.

Those omissions were repaired without changing their horticultural instructions. The packet-card pattern was then applied to the remaining entries, using transplant/tuber/slip/tree establishment fields where seed germination was inapplicable.

## Checks and practical limits

- The reusable checker validates YAML, project metadata, IDs, evidence joins, footnotes, anchors, quantity shapes, typed relations, and local paths. It passed on the canonical bundle and a standalone copy.
- Ten regression tests exercise source-join failures, missing anchors, traversal/symlink escapes, duplicate YAML keys, quantity errors, typed-relation errors, missing footnotes, and fenced-example handling.
- Skill frontmatter validation, changed-document relative-link checks, and whitespace checks passed.
- Card values were compared with owning claim statements. Full Markdown-renderer behavior, agent-consumer integration, fresh external citation auditing, and garden performance were not tested.

Next repair priorities are cultivar-specific container dimensions for the partial bag routes, local timing for mint and peas, and a complete source-fidelity pass using the rubric. These needs are preserved in the crop cards rather than hidden by the batch's structural success.
