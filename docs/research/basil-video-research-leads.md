## Topics to check

Rockwool conditioning; light and airflow; sowing; transplanting; pruning; water propagation; yellowing and pest investigation. Record which timestamp supports a specific claim if a video is reviewed. Treat a video demonstrating a task separately from evidence that it improves outcomes.

The old research essay's claims of “expert validation,” guaranteed environmental settings, immediate doubling of harvest, and a permanently vegetative production cycle are not retained as established facts.
