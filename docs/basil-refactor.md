## Deliberate editorial changes

The former prose is not preserved verbatim. Duplicate schedules were consolidated. Claims without sufficient support remain attributed assumptions or review topics. In particular, the old herb-in-oil recipe, fixed preservation times, cinnamon treatment, symptom-to-treatment shortcuts, taxonomic overreach, “expert validation,” and guaranteed/perpetual production claims are not issued as established guidance. The pinned original sources remain inspectable for future review.

No real field reports were supplied or invented. No cultivar-specific profile was created solely to populate a directory. The separate ontology example bundle remains a development fixture, not a second canonical basil profile.

Moving documents changes OKF concept IDs. Current repository links are updated; consumers of earlier IDs must retain their pinned repository revision or migrate to the destinations above. No compatibility copies of the import are retained.
