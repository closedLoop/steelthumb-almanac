# Harvest mature potatoes

Applies to [potato](../crops/potato.md). Scope: outdoor home gardening; adapt timing to local conditions. This is a focused procedure example, not a complete growing manual.

## Steps

1. For a mature harvest, wait for dried plants or full-size tubers.
2. Lift carefully and separate damaged tubers from storage candidates.[^umn-potato]
3. Record gross tuber mass, retained planting material, culls, and retained food mass separately.
4. Track later storage and preparation losses without subtracting them twice.

## Follow-up

Record whether this was a mature or early harvest. Do not eat sprouts or extensively green tubers.[^umn-potato] Raw composition below is a measurement basis, not a recommendation to eat raw potatoes.

For mass and nutrient accounting, use [the yield method](../methods/estimate-food-yield.md). Recording instructions are SteelThumb conventions.
