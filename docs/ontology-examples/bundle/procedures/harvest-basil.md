# Harvest basil leaves repeatedly

Applies to [basil](../crops/basil.md). Scope: outdoor home gardening; adapt timing to local conditions. This is a focused procedure example, not a complete growing manual.

## Steps

1. Snip young leaves as needed; cut whole stems above a pair of leaves.[^umn-basil]
2. Assign each harvest an event ID and record its date.
3. Weigh retained edible material separately from discarded stems or damaged material.
4. Sum distinct harvest events over a stated period.

## Follow-up

An event log prevents counting the same leaves twice. Fresh and dried masses stay separate.

For mass and nutrient accounting, use [the yield method](../methods/estimate-food-yield.md). Recording instructions are SteelThumb conventions.

[^umn-basil]: [Growing basil in home gardens](https://extension.umn.edu/vegetables/growing-basil).
