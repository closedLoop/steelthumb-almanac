# Plant seed potatoes

Applies to [potato](../crops/potato.md). Scope: outdoor home gardening; adapt timing to local conditions. This is a focused procedure example, not a complete growing manual.

## Steps

1. Record the cultivar and certified seed-tuber lot.
2. If dividing tubers, retain an eye on each piece and let cut surfaces dry before planting.[^umn-potato]
3. Use planting depth and spacing selected for the local setting; record both.
4. Monitor emergence and hill soil as plants develop to keep shallow tubers covered.[^umn-potato]

## Follow-up

Record material kind as seed_tuber, not true_seed. Planting dates and conditions belong to the planting record.

For mass and nutrient accounting, use [the yield method](../methods/estimate-food-yield.md). Recording instructions are SteelThumb conventions.

[^umn-potato]: [Growing potatoes in home gardens](https://extension.umn.edu/garden-and-home/yard-and-garden/gardening-in-minnesota/growing-potatoes).
