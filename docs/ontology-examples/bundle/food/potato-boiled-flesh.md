# Potatoes, boiled, cooked in skin, flesh, without salt

This is USDA SR Legacy food **Potatoes, boiled, cooked in skin, flesh, without salt**, FDC 170438. Values below use 100 g of the described edible food, not 100 g of the whole plant or a usual serving.[^fdc-170438]

Applies to [potato](../crops/potato.md) at generic food scope. **Do not relabel these as cultivar measurements.**

- Part: Tuber flesh only; cooked in skin, skin excluded from food definition.
- Preparation: boiled, without added salt.
- Source snapshot: [USDA API record](../references/data/fdc-170438.json).
- SR Legacy is historical reference data; the retrieval date is not the laboratory measurement date.
