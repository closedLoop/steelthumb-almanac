This is USDA SR Legacy food **Basil, fresh**, FDC 172232. Values below use 100 g of the described edible food, not 100 g of the whole plant or a usual serving.[^fdc-172232]

Applies to [basil](../crops/basil.md) at generic food scope. **Do not relabel these as cultivar measurements.**
