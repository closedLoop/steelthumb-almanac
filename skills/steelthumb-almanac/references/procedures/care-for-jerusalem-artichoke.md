# Care for Jerusalem artichokes

Applies to [Jerusalem artichoke](../crops/jerusalem-artichoke.md), including [grow bags](../systems/jerusalem-artichoke-grow-bags.md). Have access to water, labels for the medium and any fertilizer, and the planting record. This is an editorial synthesis, not a measured yield-improvement program.

## Routine

<a id="container-watering"></a>
1. For bags, inspect moisture daily by feel or a probe; irrigate when needed, thoroughly enough for drainage. Daily inspection is not automatic daily watering.[^water]
2. Illinois advises an aerated potting mix, drainage, and emptying collected saucer water. Check the medium before reacting to wilt.[^illinois]

<a id="support"></a>
3. Support exposed plants and water ground plantings in dry spells.[^rhs] For flexible bags, use the [support arrangement checks](../systems/jerusalem-artichoke-grow-bags.md).
4. Manage weeds with shallow cultivation or mulch after emergence, following Johnny’s guidance.[^johnny]

<a id="container-feeding"></a>
5. Read the potting-mix label for existing feed; any added fertilizer follows its own label.[^illinois] No Jerusalem-artichoke bag dose or fixed feeding interval is established here. For ground planting, Johnny’s describes ordinary vegetable-garden fertility as adequate.[^johnny]

## Follow-up

Almanac recording prompts: note moisture before watering, drainage afterward, additions and their labels, stem stability, and any leaf changes. Recheck stability after wind; investigate continuing decline using the [symptom procedure](investigate-jerusalem-artichoke.md). These checks document response without assigning a cause prematurely.

No inspected trial resolves flower removal or topping benefits for bags. This route does not require either as a yield treatment. Record any pruning as a deviation when comparing outcomes. For ending the season, use [harvest and storage](harvest-jerusalem-artichoke.md).
