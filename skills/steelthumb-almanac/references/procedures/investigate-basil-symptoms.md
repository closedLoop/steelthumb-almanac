# Investigate basil symptoms

Applies to [basil](../crops/basil.md). Record cultivar, symptom onset, distribution, both leaf surfaces, medium moisture and recent actions before choosing a cause.

## Distinguishing check and response

1. For yellowing, inspect the underside of corresponding patches and lower leaves for the signs below. Yellowing by itself is insufficient to choose a fertilizer or disease treatment.
2. If signs remain ambiguous, seek local extension diagnosis with images of both surfaces and the care history. Until then record the diagnosis as unresolved.
3. For confirmed downy mildew, use the response below. Check nearby plants and new growth on later visits; record spread, removals and pending outcomes separately.
4. Other concerns, including root decline or unidentified insects, require a matching diagnostic source. Choose any treatment only after identifying the problem and finding an applicable method.

This is a supported downy-mildew investigation route, not a complete pest encyclopedia. Additional pest or deficiency treatments need separate diagnostic evidence.

## Source settings

<a id="downy-signs-umn"></a>
Downy mildew can cause vein-bounded yellow patches and gray fluffy growth beneath leaves, beginning low on the plant.[^umn-downy]

<a id="downy-response-umn"></a>
Remove and destroy infected plants; improve spacing and keep foliage dry.[^umn-downy]
