# Harvest nasturtium leaves and flowers

Applies to [nasturtium](../crops/nasturtium.md). Garden Tropaeolum grown for edible leaves and flowers in beds or outdoor bags; cultivar unspecified.

[Outdoor grow bags](../systems/outdoor-grow-bags.md) describes the shared setup and adaptation limits.

## Before you start

Confirm the plant identity and retain any treatment history; have clean scissors and a harvest container.

## Steps and source settings

1. Choose leaves and flowers for the stated food purpose using the claim below.
2. Clip the selected material without pulling out the plant; record what was removed. This handling sequence is editorial.
3. Separate the food harvest from plants being retained for seed or display.

<a id="edible-parts"></a>
Nasturtium flowers and leaves are edible; younger leaves have better flavor than older leaves.[^uw-nasturtium]

## Follow-up

Record usable fresh mass if measured, not whole-plant biomass. Observe remaining growth before deciding on another harvest.

## Limits

No preservation process, shelf life, medicinal use, or safe harvest fraction is established. This is not guidance to eat arbitrary plants called “cress.”
