# Propagate basil from cuttings

Applies to [basil](../crops/basil.md). Identify the parent and inspect its health before selecting a shoot; unresolved disease signs go to [investigation](investigate-basil-symptoms.md).

## Steps and follow-up

Use the water-cutting method below, keeping the container labeled with parent identity and start date. Observe roots and any decay before [potting](transplant-basil.md). Record losses and later establishment, not merely the presence of roots. No minimum root length or fixed water-change interval is established by this method.

## Source settings

<a id="water-cutting-rhs"></a>
Cut a nonflowering shoot below a leaf, remove lower leaves and pinch its tip; root in water, then pot once roots form.[^rhs-basil]
