# Harvest sunflower seeds or flowers

Applies to [sunflower](../crops/sunflower.md). Annual Helianthus annuus for seed or flowers; ground culture and compact selections in outdoor bags.

[Outdoor grow bags](../systems/outdoor-grow-bags.md) describes the shared setup and adaptation limits.

## Before you start

Choose the seed or flower branch before cutting; have cutting tools, labelled containers, and a suitable drying or vase location.

## Steps and source settings

1. For seed, follow the head-readiness and drying claim below. Record drying conditions rather than assuming a fixed calendar produces dry seed.
2. For flowers, use the separate flower stage below; a cut-flower harvest is not also a mature seed harvest.
3. Separate filled seeds, empty hulls, damaged material, and any material of uncertain condition when recording output.

<a id="seed-head"></a>
For seed harvest, cut yellow-brown, drooping heads and dry them in warm, ventilated conditions before removing seeds.[^illinois-sunflower]

<a id="cut-flower"></a>
For cut flowers, harvest as ray petals begin opening, remove submerged leaves, and place stems in water.[^illinois-sunflower]

## Follow-up

Record edible kernel mass separately from heads or hulls if measuring food yield. Record flower count separately if that was the goal.

## Limits

No validated moisture target, roasting process, storage life, or seed-viability guarantee is established here. Do not use planting seed treated with crop-protection products as food.
