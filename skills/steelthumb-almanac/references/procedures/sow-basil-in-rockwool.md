# Plan basil propagation in rockwool

Applies to [basil](../crops/basil.md) in an [indoor container arrangement](../systems/indoor-basil-containers.md). This is a preparation checklist; it does not supply a complete rockwool sowing recipe. A supported [seed-compost route](sow-basil.md) is available.

## Required inputs

| Input | Decision it must resolve |
| --- | --- |
| Cube product and manufacturer propagation instructions | Initial wetting, draining and handling |
| Basil selection and seed instructions | Placement, seed count and germination conditions |
| Water analysis and intended nutrient solution | Whether pH/EC adjustment is appropriate and how to measure it |
| Fixture and tray/cover specifications | Light at seedling level, moisture and ventilation |
| Destination growing system | Transfer readiness and handling |

Obtain an applicable product-and-crop method before authoring numerical settings. A method for another crop, a fixed lamp distance or an unspecified percentage-open vent is insufficient to establish this route.

## Record and investigate

Record the selected method, products, actual conditions, sowing date, emergence and losses. If germination or seedling health is poor, inspect moisture, roots and light and use [symptom investigation](investigate-basil-symptoms.md). Preserve unresolved causes and any departures from the method.

The checklist is editorial guidance. Rockwool-specific conditioning and transfer remain research gaps; do not infer those settings from the soil or compost procedures.
