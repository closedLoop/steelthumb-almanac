# Harvest sugar snap peas

Applies to [sugar snap pea](../crops/sugar-snap-pea.md). Sugar snap peas for tender edible pods; outdoor beds and provisional bag culture, cultivar unspecified.

[Outdoor grow bags](../systems/outdoor-grow-bags.md) describes the shared setup and adaptation limits.

## Before you start

Have clean harvest containers and cooling space. Confirm these are edible-podded garden peas.

## Steps and source settings

1. Sample pods repeatedly as they fill, using the readiness claim below.
2. Support the vine while detaching each selected pod; this handling detail is editorial.
3. Label the harvest date and move the harvest to cooling promptly.

<a id="pod-readiness"></a>
Pick snap pods when nearly filled but still juicy and tender; cool promptly and remove strings if present.[^umn-peas]

## Follow-up

Record usable pod mass, picking dates, and whether the pods stayed tender. Return to [care](care-for-sugar-snap-pea.md) for declining production.

## Limits

Snow-pea flat-pod readiness is not the snap-pea target. No freezing or canning process, storage guarantee, or yield forecast is included.
