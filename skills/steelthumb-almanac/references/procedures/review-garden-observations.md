# Review garden observations

This is the Almanac project's working method for learning from a garden. It is a documentation workflow, not an experimentally validated horticultural treatment.[^steelthumb-project]

1. Record what you observed: plant identity, date, growth stage, symptoms, recent care, and relevant conditions. Include measurements or photos when available.
2. State the question and desired outcome. Keep the observation separate from your explanation of it.
3. Compare possible explanations against available sources. Preserve disagreements and missing evidence.
4. Choose a useful check or action. Record whether it is planned or completed, and which conditions changed.
5. Set a follow-up time and describe what would count as improvement, no change, or deterioration.
6. Record the outcome and reconsider the explanation. Share observations with their context; one local result does not establish general guidance.

[^steelthumb-project]: SteelThumb Almanac, “Debugging the garden.”
