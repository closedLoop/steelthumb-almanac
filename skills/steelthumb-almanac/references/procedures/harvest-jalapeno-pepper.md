# Harvest jalapeño peppers

Applies to [jalapeno pepper](../crops/jalapeno-pepper.md). Jalapeño-type Capsicum annuum for fruit; warm-season beds and outdoor grow bags, cultivar unspecified.

[Outdoor grow bags](../systems/outdoor-grow-bags.md) describes the shared setup and adaptation limits.

## Before you start

Have gloves, clean shears, and labelled harvest containers; retain cultivar and treatment history.

## Steps and source settings

1. Compare fruit size with the actual cultivar description; use the green-harvest branch below.
2. Clip fruit, separate damaged material, and label the harvest date.
3. Record green versus color-changing fruit separately if comparing use or yield.

<a id="green-harvest"></a>
Jalapeños can be picked at mature size while still green; clip the stem rather than pulling fruit off.[^umn-pepper]

<a id="handling"></a>
Wear gloves for hot-pepper harvest and wash hands before touching eyes or other sensitive areas.[^umn-pepper]

## Follow-up

Continue observing flowering and fruit set. Record usable fresh fruit mass and any losses separately.

## Limits

This procedure establishes neither Scoville heat nor a preservation recipe. Do not infer a safe canning process from pepper type or acidity.
