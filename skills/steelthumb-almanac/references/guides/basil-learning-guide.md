# Learn to grow basil

Use this sequence to grow an identified basil, observe it and explain what happened. Each linked procedure contains its own source settings.

## Evaluate the attempt

Explain the method, observations, losses, deviations and unresolved questions. Keep observations separate from explanations: a plant recovering after several changes does not identify which change caused recovery. Record procedure revision when known. These are editorial learning prompts, not evidence of gardening effectiveness.

Repeated planting is a planning choice. Harvest continuity, yield and plant longevity must be observed rather than assumed.
