# The Amazing Basil Machine

Grow, observe, harvest, and try making a replacement plant. The original “perpetual machine” curriculum is adapted here as a learning project; continuous production is a goal to investigate, not a promised result.[^curriculum]

## Learning sequence

| Stage | Practical reference | What to notice or record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meet the plant | [Basil profile](../crops/basil.md) and [variety guide](basil-varieties.md) | Which plant do you actually have? What is unknown? |
| Choose an arrangement and gather materials | [Indoor system](../systems/indoor-basil-containers.md) | Space, light, containers, medium, equipment, and constraints |
| Start seeds | [Rockwool procedure](../procedures/sow-basil-in-rockwool.md) or [growing-medium procedure](../procedures/sow-basil.md) | Sowing date, number sown, emergence, and losses |
| Move plants | [Transplanting](../procedures/transplant-basil.md) | Readiness, handling, and recovery |
| Care for plants | [Container care](../procedures/care-for-container-basil.md) | Observations before and after changes |
| Harvest | [Pruning and harvest](../procedures/harvest-basil.md) | What was removed, retained, and regrew |
| Make replacement plants | [Cuttings](../procedures/propagate-basil.md) | Roots and later establishment, including failures |
| Investigate concerns | [Symptom investigation](../procedures/investigate-basil-symptoms.md) | Observations, possible explanations, and distinguishing checks |
| Plan continuity | [Seed saving and seasonal choices](../procedures/save-basil-seed.md) | Which method fits the actual plant and season? |

Each procedure owns its numerical assumptions and review gaps. This guide intentionally links to those instructions instead of maintaining a second fertilizer, lighting, or transplant schedule.

## Learning outcomes

The old curriculum's badge asked for growing from seed, transplanting, three harvests, one successful cutting, and teaching another person. Those are learning milestones, not a scientific validation threshold.[^curriculum]

A useful completed project explains what was attempted, what happened, what remains uncertain, and what to check next. Failed or incomplete attempts can still teach something. Preserve observations separately from explanations such as “more light caused recovery.”

The earlier nursery/producer/retiree story can help explain replacement planning, but plant roles do not impose a fixed retirement age or a weekly replacement quota. Watch the actual plants.
