# Basil growing conditions

For [basil](../crops/basil.md), use the light guidance below alongside a locally appropriate frost-free planting window. Outdoor conditions and the [indoor arrangement](../systems/indoor-basil-containers.md) are different contexts.

| Decision | Maintained route |
| --- | --- |
| Sowing depth and start method | [Separate indoor and outdoor methods](../procedures/sow-basil.md) |
| Final container and planting depth | [RHS container route](../procedures/transplant-basil.md#rhs-container-planting) |
| Moisture and fertilizer | [Container care](../procedures/care-for-container-basil.md) |
| Harvest and preservation | [Harvest](../procedures/harvest-basil.md), [drying](basil-storage.md) |
| Unexplained yellowing | [Inspect before intervention](../procedures/investigate-basil-symptoms.md) |

No hydroponic EC/pH or fixture-specific light setting is established. Record the medium and equipment before choosing those settings.

## Source settings

<a id="sun-site-umn"></a>
Provide a sunny site with 6–8 hours of bright light and well-drained soil.[^umn-basil]
