# Basil species, groups, and cultivars

The former reference grouped basils by appearance, flavor, and habit. Those groupings are useful for browsing, but are not a cultivar taxonomy.[^core]

| Group in the earlier reference | Names mentioned | Identity and review needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sweet green | Genovese, Italian Large Leaf, Lettuce Leaf, Mammoth, Nufar | Confirm cultivar identity and the source of each claimed trait; the earlier text associates Nufar with Fusarium resistance. |
| Bush and dwarf | Greek bush basil, Spicy Globe | Common names and botanical rank are not interchangeable; retain supplier identity separately. |
| Purple | Dark Opal, Purple Ruffles, Red Rubin | Color grouping does not establish disease resistance or species scope. |
| Thai and other aromatic types | Thai basil, Siam Queen, Cinnamon, licorice basil | “Thai basil” is broader than the named selection Siam Queen. |
| Holy basil | Tulsi, *O. tenuiflorum*, *O. sanctum* | The earlier reference treats these separately from sweet basil; verify accepted names before a taxonomic record. |
| Lemon and lime | Lemon basil, lime basil | Species/hybrid naming in the earlier text is inconsistent and awaits taxonomic review. |
| Columnar and variegated | Pesto Perpetuo, Greek Columnar/Lesbos | Verify propagation and flowering traits for the actual selection. |
| Perennial and specialty | African Blue, tree/clove basil | The earlier text describes African Blue as a flowering sterile hybrid but elsewhere incorrectly groups it with nonflowering plants. Sterility and absence of flowering must remain separate. |

This table inventories the earlier reference's names and distinctions; it does not independently establish every identity or trait.[^core] Its underlying extension references include UF and Clemson.[^uf][^clemson]

## Choosing a profile

Start with [basil](../crops/basil.md), then [sweet basil](../crops/sweet-basil.md) where the plant's identity warrants it. Separate cultivar files should hold supported differences rather than duplicate generic care. No new cultivar-specific resistance, height, flavor, award, or yield claim is certified by this reorganization.

A seed packet is evidence for that supplier's product instructions. It does not by itself resolve disputed botanical nomenclature or establish that a general crop claim applies to every lot.
