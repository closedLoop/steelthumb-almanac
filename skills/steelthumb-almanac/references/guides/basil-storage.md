# Basil use and storage options

The earlier compilation discussed fresh culinary use, short-term cut-stem storage, freezing, drying, flavored vinegars/oils, and salt or sugar preparations. These are distinct food states and methods; shelf-life and food-safety claims require method-specific evidence.[^core]

## Options and outstanding review

| Topic | What the original material proposed | What remains unresolved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fresh use | Gentle washing, avoiding bruising, and adding basil near the end of cooking | Food handling and flavor claims need their own support. |
| Cut stems | Stems in water with submerged leaves removed; a loose cover was suggested | Storage conditions, change interval, and usable duration were not validated. |
| Refrigeration | The text discussed chilling-related discoloration | Appearance and microbiological safety are different outcomes. |
| Freezing | Leaves, basil paste, or pesto in portions | Recipe, handling, storage conditions, and retention/shelf life need a reliable food-preservation source. |
| Drying | Air drying or a dehydrator | The original time/temperature and shelf-life estimates are not endorsed here. |
| Vinegars, oils, salt, or sugar | Several home infusion/preservation suggestions | No reviewed preservation procedure is supplied by this collection. |

The original `fresh_storage_water_glass` and `freeze_paste_pesto_cubes` tasks are the documentary origins of the first two storage workflows; they are not reports of real use or demonstrated safety.[^tasks]

## Boundary

This page is a catalog of methods to review, not a complete food-preservation recipe. The old vinegar-blanching/oil-infusion directions and fixed shelf-life promises were removed from actionable guidance because this migration did not establish their support. Consult an appropriate current primary food-preservation source before adding a recipe and its storage limits.

For a field report, record the exact preparation, elapsed time, storage conditions, and observed outcome. Acceptable appearance in one report cannot establish food safety. [Harvesting](../procedures/harvest-basil.md) covers the growing task; food composition profiles must separately identify fresh, dried, or prepared food and their mass basis.
