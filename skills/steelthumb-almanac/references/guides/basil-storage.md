# Basil use and storage options

For harvested [basil](../crops/basil.md), this page provides one preservation route rather than mixing recipes.

## Dehydrator route

Discard damaged or dirty material, rinse under cool running water and shake off excess. Follow the temperature and endpoint settings below. NCHFP gives a typical 1–4 hours, with periodic checks; actual drying determines completion. Basil is a high-moisture herb that can mold if drying is too slow.[^nchfp-herbs]

Label the finished batch and record method, temperature and storage conditions. If the endpoint is uncertain, continue checking rather than treating elapsed time as proof of dryness.

## Source settings

<a id="dehydrator-herbs-nchfp"></a>
Rinse and drain herbs; dry in one layer at 95–115°F, up to 125°F in humid conditions.[^nchfp-herbs]

<a id="dry-endpoint-nchfp"></a>
Finished leaves crumble and stems snap when bent; store dried herbs airtight in a cool, dry, dark place.[^nchfp-herbs]
