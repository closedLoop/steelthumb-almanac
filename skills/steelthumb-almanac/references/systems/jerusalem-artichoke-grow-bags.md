# Jerusalem artichokes in grow bags

This system means a freestanding outdoor fabric grow bag filled with container medium. It is separate from a bed, a rigid pot, or a shallow bag of compost laid flat. It applies to the [Jerusalem artichoke crop](../crops/jerusalem-artichoke.md).

<a id="fabric-drying"></a>
Fabric loses moisture faster than plastic or glazed containers.[^water] The [care procedure](../procedures/care-for-jerusalem-artichoke.md) therefore uses moisture observations rather than a fixed irrigation timer.

## Choose and document the arrangement

Use the [planting procedure](../procedures/plant-jerusalem-artichoke.md#container-dimensions) for dimensions, material, and planting count. This is an editorial adaptation of pot guidance; no inspected controlled trial establishes the minimum or optimum fabric-bag volume. Measure the filled bag's width and usable medium depth. A nominal litre or gallon label alone cannot establish that both dimensions are met.

<a id="pail-alternative"></a>
USask instead suggests a 5-gallon pail, while acknowledging container-growing difficulty.[^usask] This is a separate recommendation, not the lower endpoint of a proven size range. Its gallon convention is unspecified. We select the dimension-based route because it is easier to check against an actual bag; this does not prove superior yield.

## Practical adaptations to check locally

The following are Almanac precautions and observation prompts, not trial findings:

- Choose a location where the bag, drainage, and surrounding surface can be inspected. Do not assume fabric, seams, or drainage openings guarantee containment.
- Plan support independent of a flexible rim; check stability after filling and as the canopy develops.
- Keep drainage unobstructed. Record bag material, dimensions, medium, tuber count, cultivar if known, sunlight, and support arrangement.
- At harvest, inspect the bag and sort its contents on a contained surface. Check the former location for volunteers the following spring.

Follow [planting](../procedures/plant-jerusalem-artichoke.md), [care](../procedures/care-for-jerusalem-artichoke.md), [harvest](../procedures/harvest-jerusalem-artichoke.md), and [problem investigation](../procedures/investigate-jerusalem-artichoke.md). Exposed-bag winter survival and escape prevention remain evidence gaps. Do not transfer in-ground hardiness claims to a bag without local evidence.
