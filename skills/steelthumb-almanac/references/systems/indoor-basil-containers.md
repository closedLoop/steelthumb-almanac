# Indoor basil in containers

This arrangement connects an indoor seed-starting or cutting area with containers for leaf harvest. Identify the basil, growing medium, container dimensions, lighting fixture and fertilizer before choosing settings.

## Set up and use

1. Choose a bright growing position using [growing conditions](../guides/basil-growing-conditions.md). For artificial light, use the actual fixture's instructions and measure light at plant level; no generic lamp distance is supplied.
2. Select drained containers and medium using [transplanting](../procedures/transplant-basil.md). Keep the drainage outlet and surrounding surface accessible for inspection.
3. Start in [seed compost](../procedures/sow-basil.md) or use [water cuttings](../procedures/propagate-basil.md).
4. Follow [care](../procedures/care-for-container-basil.md), [harvest](../procedures/harvest-basil.md) and [symptom investigation](../procedures/investigate-basil-symptoms.md).

## Checks and limits

Record moisture and drainage before watering, and inspect leaf condition and new growth. Investigate persistent wilting, yellowing or collapse before changing multiple inputs. This assembly is editorial; the linked procedures own its factual settings.

[Rockwool propagation](../procedures/sow-basil-in-rockwool.md) needs its own product-specific inputs. No fixed replacement quota, retirement age, continuous yield or lighting optimum is established. Record actual conditions and outcomes for each attempt.
