# Basil research sources

Sources inspected on **2026-09-27**. Follow each claim’s source ID and passage locator. These are gardening recommendations, supplier-independent references and food-preservation guidance; none records an Almanac field trial.

## umn-basil

[Growing basil in home gardens — Minnesota Extension](https://extension.umn.edu/garden-and-home/yard-and-garden/gardening-in-minnesota/yard-and-garden-problems/growing-basil). Marissa Schuh and Shirley Mah Kooyman; page reviewed 2026. Introduction, fertility, direct seeding and harvesting. Container feeding is product-relative; outdoor row guidance is not bag density.

## uf-basil

[Basil — UF/IFAS Gardening Solutions](https://gardeningsolutions.ifas.ufl.edu/plants/edibles/vegetables/basil/). UF/IFAS Gardening Solutions; date not supplied. Characteristics and Problems. Compact selection examples retained. The all-sweet-basils-susceptible statement conflicts with Minnesota’s named resistant selections and is not adopted.

## clemson-basil

[Basil — Clemson Extension](https://hgic.clemson.edu/factsheet/basil/). HGIC 1327, updated 2019-05-01. Introduction, Harvesting & Storage, Varieties and Problems. Disease resistance is pathogen-specific. Wet-weather harvesting advice is risk reduction, not a measured outcome in this bundle.

## umn-downy

[Basil downy mildew — Minnesota Extension](https://extension.umn.edu/agriculture/specialty-crops/vegetable-farming/disease-management/basil-downy-mildew). Marissa Schuh and Michelle Grabowski; reviewed by Natalie Hoidal in 2022. Identification signs, home-garden cultural controls and resistance table. Named resistance evidence supersedes blanket susceptibility generalizations.

## nchfp-herbs

[Drying herbs — National Center for Home Food Preservation](https://nchfp.uga.edu/how/dry/recipes/herbs/). University of Georgia; extracted from So Easy to Preserve, sixth edition, 2014, revised by Elizabeth L. Andress and Judy A. Harrison. Dehydrator conditions, dryness endpoint and storage. Drying endpoint takes precedence over elapsed time.

## rhs-basil

[How to grow basil — RHS Advice Team](https://www.rhs.org.uk/herbs/basil/grow-your-own). RHS Advice Team; date not supplied. Sowing indoors, Planting, Watering and Propagating. UK guidance; surface sowing and container planting are separate from Minnesota ground sowing. Fabric use is an adaptation.

## ssc-basil

[Basil — Seed Savers Connect; Liz Worth, Julie Davies, Nellie Pryke](https://www.seedsavers.org.au/2025/09/14/basil/). Seed Savers Connect; Liz Worth, Julie Davies and Nellie Pryke; 2025-09-14. Seed maturity, Harvest, Processing and Storage. Identity synonyms and isolation-distance generalizations are not adopted. CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 source: factual synthesis only, no copied wording or images.

## Scope and reuse

The [basil hub](../crops/basil.md) links the maintained guidance. Rockwool product settings, exact cultivar maturity and local fabric-bag outcomes remain unresolved. A retrieved page does not establish that every statement on it is current or supported. No external photos, tables or full-text copies are included. Source owners retain their rights; document-level license metadata identifies the Almanac prose license.
