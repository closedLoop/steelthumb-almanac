## Disagreements and exclusions

- Container recommendations differ; the [system document](../systems/jerusalem-artichoke-grow-bags.md#pail-alternative) preserves both. No inspected source establishes bag yield or optimal bag volume.
- The [planting procedure](../procedures/plant-jerusalem-artichoke.md#site) preserves the light disagreement. These recommendations are not measured light-response curves.
- The USask storage tab contains an apparent carrot carryover and generic preservation links; neither supports Jerusalem-artichoke shelf life or a tested preservation recipe.[^usask]
- Johnny’s nutrition commentary was not adopted as health guidance.[^johnny] No primary food-composition record was inspected; a FoodProfile and YieldEstimate are deliberately absent.
- Cultivar performance, pruning benefits, bag winter protection, and complete disease guidance need further work. Unresolved topics are not zero values or evidence of no effect.

Search results from retailers, videos, and informal grower accounts were discovery leads only and were not adopted as bag trial evidence. No report was manufactured from those leads. The installed collection contains no source photos, copied tables, or full-text archives; these are original concise summaries and links. External source content retains its owners' rights; original Almanac prose uses the repository's MIT license.
