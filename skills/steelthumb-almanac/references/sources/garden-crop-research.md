# Garden crop source catalog

Sources accessed on 2026-09-27 for the [crop collection](../index.md). Source IDs join the footnotes and claim evidence in individual documents. This catalog records documentary origins, not garden trials or verification events. Dates not shown remain unrecorded; access does not pin a mutable page revision.

## Attribution and reuse

The Almanac prose is an original synthesis under MIT. External articles, photographs, tables, and illustrations remain under their publishers’ terms; none are bundled here. Source titles and links retain credit. Maryland Grows bag accounts are attributed reports, not newly submitted Almanac field reports or independent replications.

## Coverage and decisions

| Topic | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | Named Yukon Gold is a Cultivar; russet and cherry tomato remain crop categories. “Dwarf lime” has separate Persian and Key branches. |
| Establishment and care | Each crop hub links to task procedures, including symptom checks and follow-up. Pepper and tomato start with transplants, sweet potato with slips, citrus with nursery trees. |
| Bags | Explicit bag accounts are distinguished from rigid-container adaptations. Unestablished capacities and densities remain gaps. |
| Source differences | Pea spacing methods, nasturtium feeding contexts, potato storage, sweet-potato harvest/curing, and citrus picking stages are retained separately in the owning procedures. |
| Unresolved | Local calendars, most cultivar-specific bag performance, fertilizer doses, and long-term citrus fabric culture are not established. |
| Out of scope | Quantified nutrient profiles, yield forecasts, preservation recipes, and invented field outcomes. |

Regional extension and RHS advice is useful guidance, not proof that an assembled procedure has been tested in every region or container. Record the actual material, cultivar, conditions, and outcome when applying it.
