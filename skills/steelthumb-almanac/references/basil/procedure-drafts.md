# Basil procedure drafts

These are unreviewed instruction templates extracted from SteelThumb’s task catalog, with original task IDs retained as section locators.[^steelthumb-tasks] They are candidate gardening procedures, not verified treatments or records of completed work. The original robot scenarios, success criteria, task IDs, and executable templates remain in SteelThumb.

Scope: indoor basil, rockwool germination, soil producers in 6-inch pots, water-rooted cuttings, timed LED lighting, and gentle airflow. Cultivar and fertilizer product are unspecified.[^steelthumb-procedural]

Review [parameter assumptions and disagreements](growing-parameters.md) before using these drafts. Diagnostic shortcuts, pesticide instructions, and food-storage advice require source review. Read the [attribution and license](attribution.md).

## condition_rockwool

Condition rockwool to pH 5.5-6.0

1. Fill a bowl with water and adjust to pH 5.5-6.0.
2. Submerge rockwool cubes 15-30 minutes, then drain.

## setup_germination_tray

Set up germination tray (light + airflow)

1. Place conditioned cubes in tray and cover with humidity dome.
2. Position LED ~6 in (15 cm) above tray; set 14h ON / 10h OFF.
3. Set fan to gentle airflow.

## sow_basil_seeds

Sow basil seeds in conditioned cubes

1. Drop 1-2 seeds into each cube hole and pinch closed lightly.
2. Mist surface evenly; open dome vent to ~20-40%.
3. Label date/cultivar.

## daily_seedling_check

Daily seedling check (moisture/vent)

1. Check moisture; mist if surface is dry-light, not soggy.
2. Adjust vents to avoid heavy condensation.
3. Confirm light remains ~6 in above canopy.

## remove_humidity_dome

Remove humidity dome at sprout

1. When cotyledons emerge, remove dome.
2. Increase gentle airflow.

## thin_seedlings

Thin seedlings in cube

1. Identify strongest sprout and snip extras at media level.

## transplant_cube_to_pot

Transplant rooted cube to 6-inch pot (soil + perlite)

1. Fill pot with 80% soil / 20% perlite; make hole to cube height.
2. Seat whole cube; backfill gently.
3. Water to slight runoff; drain saucer.

## early_establishment_check

First 10-14 day establishment check (no feed yet)

1. Verify new growth.
2. Water only when top 1 in (2-3 cm) is dry.

## routine_watering

Routine watering (container)

1. Check top 1 in for dryness.
2. Water to slight runoff; drain saucer within 10 min.

## half_strength_fertilization

Half-strength fertilization (monthly)

1. Mix fertilizer at half label rate.
2. Apply to pre-moistened soil; drain excess.

## leach_salts

Leach salts (if crust present)

1. Flush pot with 2-3× pot volume of water.
2. Drain thoroughly.

## adjust_light_distance

Light distance correction (etiolation)

1. Assess stretch/lean and set light 6-12 in above canopy.
2. Rotate pot 90°.

## monthly_light_position_maintenance

Monthly light/position maintenance

1. Verify light 6-12 in above canopy.
2. Rotate pot 90° for symmetry.

## pinch_apical_tip

Pinch apical tip above node (bushiness)

1. Locate top node and cut ~0.5 in above it.

## prune_weekly

Weekly shaping prune (≤1/3 canopy)

1. Snip above nodes across several stems; remove ≤1/3 canopy.
2. Rotate pot 90°.

## remove_flower_buds

Emergency flower bud removal (anti-bolt)

1. Pinch off all emerging buds.

## gray_mold_prevention

Gray mold prevention after heavy prune

1. Avoid overhead watering for 48 h post-prune.
2. Increase airflow.

## take_basil_cutting

Take basil cutting for cloning (4-6 inches)

1. Choose non-flowering stem; cut below a node.
2. Strip lower leaves; place stem in clean water; label.

## root_cutting_in_water

Maintain water cuttings (oxygen & hygiene)

1. Keep leaves above water line.
2. Replace water every 2-3 days until roots ≥1-2 in.

## pot_up_rooted_cutting

Pot up rooted cutting to soil

1. Plant rooted cutting in moist mix with leaves above surface.
2. Water gently to settle.

## humidity_tent_for_transplant

Humidity tent for fresh transplants (24-48h)

1. Cover loosely to raise RH for 24-48 h.
2. Remove once turgid recovery is stable.

## rinse_leaves

Clean leaf rinse (IPM baseline)

1. Rinse leaf undersides with lukewarm water.
2. Air-dry with gentle fan.

## spray_insecticidal_soap

Aphid/whitefly response (soap spray)

1. Rinse leaves.
2. Spray diluted insecticidal soap on undersides/tops.
3. Recheck in ~72 h; repeat if needed.

## humidity_increase_for_mites

Spider mite response (humidity + soap)

1. Increase local humidity; rinse webbing.
2. Apply soap; avoid hot light windows.

## slug_snail_exclusion

Slug/snail exclusion (container)

1. Apply iron-phosphate bait or copper tape barrier.
2. Elevate pot if needed.

## downy_mildew_cull

Downy mildew triage (indoors)

1. Identify angular yellow lesions; confirm gray/purple underside.
2. Bag entire plant; remove and trash.
3. Sanitize tools and surfaces.

## fusarium_wilt_disposal

Fusarium wilt triage

1. Confirm vascular browning on cut stem with soil still moist.
2. Cull plant; discard soil; do not replant basil in same medium.

## nutrient_deficiency_fix

Nutrient deficiency correction (likely N-low)

1. Identify uniform yellowing on older leaves.
2. Apply half-strength balanced fertilizer.

## magnesium_deficiency_fix

Magnesium deficiency correction (containers)

1. Identify interveinal yellowing on newer leaves.
2. Apply dilute Mg supplement per label.

## rotate_pot

Rotate pot 90° weekly

1. Rotate pot 90° once per week.

## temperature_rh_monitoring

Temperature/RH monitoring & adjust

1. Keep veg temp ~65-75°F (18-24°C) and gentle airflow.
2. Adjust fan/tent as needed.

## harvest_basil

Weekly harvest for kitchen use

1. Select tender tops and cut above nodes, removing ≤1/3 canopy.
2. Rinse harvest as needed.

## harvest_batch_for_pesto

Large batch harvest for pesto

1. Distribute cuts across multiple plants; leave ≥2 leaf pairs per stem.

## fresh_storage_water_glass

Fresh storage (counter water-glass method)

1. Strip lower leaves; place stems in water glass.
2. Loosely cover with bag at room temp.

## freeze_paste_pesto_cubes

Freezing as paste/pesto cubes

1. Blend leaves with a little oil.
2. Fill ice tray; freeze; bag and label.

## end_of_season_cuttings

End-of-season continuity (pre-frost)

1. Take 4-6 cuttings from best plants; root indoors under lights.
2. Compost healthy leftovers; trash diseased material.

## sanitation_cycle

Sanitation loop (tools & area)

1. Wipe shears with alcohol before/after use.
2. Clear debris from trays and surfaces.
