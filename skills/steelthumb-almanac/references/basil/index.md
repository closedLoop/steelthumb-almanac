# Basil: imported reference and procedure drafts

This collection holds reusable basil material migrated from SteelThumb on 2026-09-27. All documents are **unreviewed drafts**. Source citations were retained; their presence does not establish claim accuracy. The crop/cultivar ontology examples elsewhere in the repository remain separate development examples.

- [Cultivation reference](core-facts.md): varieties, growing conditions, propagation, care, pests, diseases, harvest, and storage.
- [Learning guide](learning-guide.md): The Amazing Basil Machine curriculum.
- [Video research](video-research.md): source and teaching notes supporting the curriculum.
- [Procedure drafts](procedure-drafts.md): extracted gardening instructions, indexed by original task ID.
- [Growing parameters](growing-parameters.md): imported assumptions and unresolved disagreements.
- [Attribution and license](attribution.md): source revision, migration record, and CC-BY-SA-4.0 terms.

Robot execution, simulation models, success criteria, and training plans remain in [SteelThumb](https://github.com/closedloop-technologies/steelthumb/tree/main/steelthumb_v1_basil). These references contain no newly researched nutrition values or observed yield measurements.
