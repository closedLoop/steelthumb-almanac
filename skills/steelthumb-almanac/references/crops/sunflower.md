# Annual sunflowers

<a id="identity"></a>
Helianthus annuus is the annual sunflower grown for flowers and seeds.[^umn-sunflower]

Annual Helianthus annuus for seed or flowers; ground culture and compact selections in outdoor bags.

## Quick reference

Seed-packet-style summary; linked claims retain the source and growing conditions.

| Field | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| Start with | Annual sunflower seed selected for food seed or cut flowers; retain mature-height information. |
| When / depth | [After frost danger passes; sow 1 in deep](../procedures/plant-sunflower.md#depth). |
| Spacing | [Giant types: about 2 ft apart](../procedures/plant-sunflower.md#giant-spacing). Use packet spacing for the selected dwarf cultivar. |
| Light / support | [Full sun and well-drained ground](../procedures/plant-sunflower.md#site-light); [stake or shelter tall, heavy-headed plants](../procedures/care-for-sunflower.md#wind-support). |
| Emergence / maturity | Obtain germination and maturity estimates for the named cultivar; flower and seed harvest have different endpoints. |
| Water | [Water in dry weather; control weeds early](../procedures/care-for-sunflower.md#dry-weather). |
| Harvest | [Flowers: ray petals just opening](../procedures/harvest-sunflower.md#cut-flower). [Seeds: yellow-brown, drooping heads, followed by drying](../procedures/harvest-sunflower.md#seed-head). |
| Grow bag | [Choose a dwarf container selection](../procedures/plant-sunflower.md#dwarf-bag). [Junior dimensions and density](../procedures/plant-sunflower.md#packet-container) supply a flower-growing pot route; fabric performance and food-seed yield are untested. |
