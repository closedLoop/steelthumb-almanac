# Culinary mint

<a id="clonal-identity"></a>
Mint seedlings may not retain cultivar identity; use known plants, divisions, or cuttings for a named selection.[^usu-mint]

Culinary spearmint and peppermint for leaves; identified plants in beds or outdoor containers.
