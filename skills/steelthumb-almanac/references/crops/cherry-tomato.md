# Cherry tomatoes

<a id="habit"></a>
Cherry tomatoes include both dwarf and tall-growing cultivars; small fruit does not imply a small plant.[^umd-tomato]

Cherry-fruited Solanum lycopersicum for fresh fruit; ground and outdoor bag growing, cultivar unspecified.
