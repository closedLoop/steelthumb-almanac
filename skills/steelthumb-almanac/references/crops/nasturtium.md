# Garden nasturtium

<a id="identity"></a>
Garden nasturtiums include Tropaeolum majus and T. minus, distinct from watercress.[^uw-nasturtium]

Garden Tropaeolum grown for edible leaves and flowers in beds or outdoor bags; cultivar unspecified.
