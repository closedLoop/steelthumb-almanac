# Sugar snap peas

<a id="identity"></a>
Sugar snap peas are edible-podded Pisum sativum, harvested with developed peas and tender pods.[^umn-peas]

Sugar snap peas for tender edible pods; outdoor beds and provisional bag culture, cultivar unspecified.
