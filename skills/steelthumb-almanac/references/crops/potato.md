# Potatoes

<a id="identity"></a>
Potato, Solanum tuberosum, produces edible underground stem tubers.[^isu-potato]

Solanum tuberosum for edible tubers in soil and grow bags; general guidance with separate cultivar scope.

## Quick reference

Seed-packet-style summary; linked claims retain the source and growing conditions.

| Field | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| Start with | [Certified seed potatoes](../procedures/plant-potato.md#seed-stock); this route uses small whole tubers. |
| When to plant | [Soil at least 45°F](../procedures/plant-potato.md#warm-soil) (Maryland). |
| Ground depth | [About 4 in deep](../procedures/plant-potato.md#ground-depth) (Iowa). |
| Ground spacing | [About 1 ft within rows](../procedures/plant-potato.md#ground-spacing); [2–3 ft between rows](../procedures/plant-potato.md#ground-row-spacing). |
| Light / habit | [Sunny, well-drained site](../procedures/plant-potato.md#site-light); [keep developing tubers covered](../procedures/care-for-potato.md#cover-tubers). |
| Emergence / maturity | Emergence interval and local maturity are unestablished; obtain the cultivar’s planting-based estimate and use skin readiness below. |
| Water | [Maintain water supply during tuber development without excess](../procedures/care-for-potato.md#moisture). |
| Harvest | [For storage: vines died back and skins resist rubbing](../procedures/harvest-potato.md#mature-skin). |
| Grow bag | [Spudnik: 16 in deep × 16 in across](../procedures/plant-potato.md#bag-dimensions), [nominal 12 gallons](../procedures/plant-potato.md#bag-capacity), [3–5 seed tubers/pieces](../procedures/plant-potato.md#bag-count). Reported method, not a cultivar optimum; [follow its staged filling recipe](../procedures/plant-potato.md#base-layer). |
