# Dwarf lime trees

<a id="persian-identity"></a>
Tahiti, Persian, and Bearss are names used for Citrus latifolia.[^uf-persian]

<a id="key-identity"></a>
Key or Mexican lime is Citrus aurantifolia.[^uf-key]

Compact nursery lime trees: separate Persian/Tahiti/Bearss and Key/Mexican branches until the plant label resolves identity.

## Quick reference

Seed-packet-style summary; linked claims retain the source and growing conditions.

| Field | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| Start with | Labelled nursery tree; record scion/selection, rootstock if grafted, and root-ball size. |
| When outdoors | [After frost risk passes; shelter before frost and change light gradually](../procedures/care-for-dwarf-citrus.md#seasonal-move). |
| Planting depth | [Crown at the medium surface](../procedures/plant-dwarf-citrus.md#plant-depth). |
| Light | [At least 6 h direct light daily](../procedures/plant-dwarf-citrus.md#light) (Maryland container guidance). |
| Size / support | [Dwarfing rootstock can keep a grafted tree compact](../procedures/plant-dwarf-citrus.md#rootstock). Obtain its expected size and check container stability. |
| Establishment / fruiting | Seed germination is not applicable to this nursery-tree route. Time to first fruit is unestablished for an unidentified tree; confirm nursery age and bearing history. |
| Water / feed | [Let the surface dry, then water thoroughly and drain](../procedures/care-for-dwarf-citrus.md#water) (Meyer guidance; lime adaptation). [Feed by label during active growth](../procedures/care-for-dwarf-citrus.md#feed). |
| Container / bag | [UC: 14–16-inch starter pots for small trees](../procedures/plant-dwarf-citrus.md#pot-size); repot when root-bound. Fabric dimensions and long-term suitability are unresolved; use a supported movable-pot route or obtain rootstock-specific bag advice. |
| Harvest | [Persian: about 45 mm diameter and dark to medium-dark green](../procedures/harvest-dwarf-citrus.md#persian-stage); [Key: mature yellow peel](../procedures/harvest-dwarf-citrus.md#key-stage) (UF branches). |
