# Jerusalem artichoke (sunchoke)

<a id="accepted-name"></a>
*Helianthus tuberosus* L. is accepted in Asteraceae.[^kew]

<a id="edible-tuber-perennial"></a>
Sunchoke is a perennial sunflower harvested for its underground tubers; globe artichoke is a different crop harvested for flower buds.[^mu] This profile covers outdoor food growing, with cultivar and user region unspecified. It does not cover ornamental sunflowers or infer cultivar identity from tuber appearance.

<a id="persistent-spread"></a>
Its persistent spreading habit makes site choice consequential.[^mu] Choose either the [ground planting route](../procedures/plant-jerusalem-artichoke.md) or the [grow-bag system](../systems/jerusalem-artichoke-grow-bags.md) before obtaining planting material. A bag is not documented here as an escape-proof barrier.
